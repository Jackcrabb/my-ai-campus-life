# Production boy wardrobe artwork

Original artwork generated with OpenAI ImageGen for the approved crafted-pixel visual direction. These assets are fictional student depictions inspired by published Hong Kong school uniform descriptions; no school logos or crests are reproduced.

## Production files

- `dist/assets/characters/students-boy-queens-college.png` — Queen’s College summer shirt and white long trousers. Source generation `exec-18766b8f-b02f-4e58-a2f3-39f6b5cb280c`.
- `dist/assets/characters/students-boy-hkcwcc.png` — Hong Kong Chinese Women’s Club College winter white long-sleeve shirt and grey long trousers. Source generation `exec-795e1f29-3851-4509-a19d-3516b21e94a8`.

Both PNGs are 1536 × 1024 RGBA originals. No pixel editing, background removal, or synthetic recolouring was applied. Alpha and frame bounds were inspected read-only. Production cropping and foot registration are documented in `dist/assets/characters/atlas-layout.json` and consumed by the canvas renderer.

## Selection and animation contract

Each sheet contains six columns and three rows. Columns 0–2 use a short side part with light, medium, and deep skin tones; columns 3–5 use a short textured crop with the same skin tones. Row 0 is idle and rows 1–2 provide alternating walking poses. The renderer uses measured source bounds rather than assuming perfectly equal generated cell spacing. Every player variant uses long trousers; no ponytail, skirt, or dress is included on these sheets.

The outfits follow the project’s verified school-uniform source record. The second outfit is supported by the [HKCWCC official school uniform page](https://www.hkcwcc.edu.hk/en/school-uniform). The uniform choices are illustrations for this educational game, without endorsement by the schools.

## Art direction reference

`design/art-options/version-1-crafted-pixel.png`, the user-selected first option, was supplied as the style reference. Generated files were visually inspected for full feet, distinct short hair, appropriate outfits, and alpha transparency.
