# 香港中學課程對照與題庫設計 / Curriculum alignment and question design

Version: `hk-curriculum-2026-10-v2`. Official-source check: **10 October 2026**.

The replacement bank contains **234 independently authored questions: six year groups × thirteen game spaces × three questions**. Each question has its own task, three meaningful choices, answer-specific feedback and a learning takeaway in Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese and English. Stable IDs and the original 1/2/3 subject-star structure remain unchanged. Each year still has 39 subject questions and a maximum of 78 subject stars. The four ethical-AI cases and transfer task are unchanged.

## What this alignment means

這是一套依香港課程範疇設計的原創、精選形成性評估題庫，不是教育局／考評局官方題庫、公開試真題、完整課程或統一的中一至中六教學進度。年級安排是本項目的教學排序；教師應按校本課程、學生先備知識、語文需要和修讀科目調整。三個星級表示題目的設計要求，並不標籤學生能力，也未經學生試測建立難度或效度。

This is an original curated formative bank informed by Hong Kong curriculum domains. It is **not** an official bank, a collection of past-paper items, complete syllabus coverage or a prescribed annual teaching sequence. EDB guides often specify key-stage expectations rather than assigning every concept to a particular school year. Our annual placement is an editorial decision, subject to teacher review. No empirical difficulty, attainment equivalence, learner efficacy or independent native-speaker review is claimed.

The prior bank had 78 base scenarios with the same three forms: answer the question, reject an incorrect AI suggestion, and select an evidence-based justification. The new bank replaces those repeated forms with **234 distinct problems**. Level 1 focuses on a concept or interpretation; level 2 requires application; level 3 emphasises inference, design, comparison or evaluation. This is a design intention, not a psychometrically validated difficulty scale. Some senior topics remain introductory within their key stage.

## Subject and cohort boundaries

- **Chinese and English:** reading evidence, language in context, audience/purpose, coherent writing, evaluation and synthesis. Original Chinese excerpts remain in Chinese when the English interface is selected, because those items assess Chinese reading; English source sentences remain in English in the Chinese interfaces. Interface translation does not translate away the language skill being assessed. Chinese classical-style excerpts are newly authored teaching passages, not quotations attributed to historical authors.
- **Mathematics:** junior number, algebra, geometry, data and probability; senior compulsory-part connections include functions, equations, exponential/logarithmic reasoning, circle geometry, probability, dispersion and linear programming. The bank does not claim M1/M2 coverage. Conditional probability is handled directly, without requiring Bayes' theorem. The illustrative ramp ratio is an invented mathematics condition, not a building-accessibility rule.
- **Science/Chemistry room:** S1–S3 intentionally covers selected integrated Science ideas, including measurement, cells, electricity, matter, energy and inquiry. S4–S6 uses selected **Chemistry elective** concepts. The room does not imply all pupils take Chemistry or that biology/physics are fully covered. Short text items cannot assess practical laboratory competence.
- **Computing:** junior Technology Education/computational thinking and selected senior **ICT elective** concepts. Data, formulas, algorithms, representation, databases, networks, security and model evaluation replace an exclusively AI-reliability focus. This is not the complete ICT compulsory/elective syllabus.
- **Geography, History, Music, Visual Arts and PE:** junior/general learning is distinguished from selected senior elective or enrichment concepts. Chinese History and History are separate subjects; the shared History room does not cover the complete Chinese History curriculum. Text questions cannot assess an entire artwork, music performance, historical essay or physical performance.
- **Hall:** junior connections are to Citizenship, Economics and Society (CES); S4–S6 is civic/economic enquiry enrichment, not a claimed continuation of junior CES or complete Citizenship and Social Development / Economics exam preparation.
- **Library and rooftop:** these are interdisciplinary information-literacy and environmental-enquiry spaces, not separate compulsory HKDSE subjects. Rooftop tasks connect Science, Geography, numeracy and project learning. Senior uncertainty, life-cycle boundaries and comparison-group tasks are enrichment applications.

The updated junior Science framework permits earlier piloting and begins phased S1 implementation in **2027/28**. The revised junior Geography framework also starts with S1 by **2027/28**, with piloting encouraged in 2026/27. These frameworks are used as forward-looking connections, not assumed to be the taught syllabus of every current class. Geography senior supplementary notes apply from 2025/26. The optimised senior History framework is not automatically applied to present S4–S6 cohorts; see the humanities companion for 2027/28 S4 and 2030 assessment cautions. [Science implementation circular](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/kla/science-edu/jss/EDBC25014E.pdf), [Geography implementation circular](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/renewal/CM/EDBC26002E.pdf), [senior Geography supplement](https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/en/curriculum-development/renewal/CM/EDBCM24255E.pdf).

## Classroom use and assessment

Select a small route to match the taught unit. Completing every room is optional for a lesson, even though the game exit has its own star requirement. Introduce relevant vocabulary and allow pupils to explain reasoning orally, with diagrams or in writing after the choice. Use incorrect-option feedback to discuss the misconception, then retry; do not equate repeated-game stars with a formal examination grade.

For mixed-language classes, keep the assessed source language where language itself is the target, while offering interface language, vocabulary support and teacher discussion. For SEN/NCS learners, adapt reading load, response format, pacing and prerequisite content through the teacher tools. The bank has not been validated for every learner profile and is not an adapted-curriculum certification. [EDB Secondary Education Curriculum Guide](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/curriculum-development/major-level-of-edu/secondary/CG_documents.html), [EDB adapted senior curriculum resources](https://www.edb.gov.hk/en/curriculum-development/curriculum-area/special-educational-needs/ssc/index.html).

The original MEDD8934 purpose remains ethical and inclusive AI practices in language education. The four cases provide the main AI evidence–decision–repair–reflection route; these subject rooms provide a broader campus learning context. They do not replace a real AI-tool demonstration required by the course presentation. Authored game feedback is not a live AI response.

Suggested pilot: a teacher checks the target unit and three items before class; 4–6 pupils complete a short route while the teacher observes vocabulary barriers, reasoning and wrong-option interpretations; revise ambiguous items; then compare a brief unaided pre/post explanation of one comparable problem. Record actual duration, accessibility issues and transfer explanations. This pilot is **planned, not completed**; no learner-outcome claim follows from automated tests.

## Authoring and verification

- Edit the compact files under `scripts/content-authoring/grade-items-*.json`, keeping each `(subjectId, grade, level)` unique. Each option carries its own explanation and the canonical correct option is explicit. The obsolete scenario-template file is retained only as development history and is not read by the generator.
- Run `python scripts/content-authoring/build-grade-bank.py` in an authoring environment with `opencc-python-reimplemented`. OpenCC is an authoring-only conversion dependency; no new browser/server dependency is deployed. Traditional/English are authored together; Simplified Chinese is generated with `t2s`.
- The generator rejects duplicate/missing items and repeated complete stems, balances correct positions across each room/year, and writes the server JSON, browser mirror and content-hash/version record. `data/curriculum-version.json` identifies the bank; its SHA-256 hashes the UTF-8 `grade-bank.json` file including its final newline.
- Run `node tests/grade-bank.test.cjs` and `node tests/curriculum-bank.test.cjs`. These check completeness, language fields, option rotation, unchanged scoring, mirror parity, original-versus-repeated stems, mapping integrity, version hash and selected independently recomputed numerical answers. They do not establish pedagogical effectiveness or independent translation accuracy.
- The server's separate versioned seed-refresh mechanism preserves earlier assigned revisions and teacher-customised questions. Publishing this new starter bank must not silently rewrite a pupil's existing assignment or a teacher's edited item.

Current limitations: only three questions per subject/year, limited topic sampling, text-heavy MCQ format, teacher-dependent sequencing and no empirical calibration. The teacher should retain fuller practical, oral, extended-response and authentic assessment alongside this game.
